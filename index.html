ⓘ Add a trash reminder

🍎 iPhone — Apple Reminders

  1. Open the Reminders app and tap New Reminder.
  2. Enter Take out the trash (or your name + trash duty).
  3. Tap the ⓘ / details button and turn on Date.
  4. Choose the Saturday you are responsible for.
  5. Tap Repeat → Custom → Every 7 weeks.
  6. Save the reminder.

🤖 Android — Google Calendar / Tasks

  1. Open Google Calendar (or Google Tasks).
  2. Create a task/reminder called Take out the trash.
  3. Set the date to your assigned Saturday.
  4. Choose Does not repeat → Custom.
  5. Set it to repeat every 7 weeks.
  6. Save the task.
Tip: The calendar on this page shows the rotation. Your phone reminder is simply an extra notification so you don't forget your assigned Saturday.
