ⓘ Add a trash reminder

🍎 iPhone — Apple Calendar

  1. Choose your name in Phone reminder.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar.
  3. iPhone opens the calendar event. Tap Add.
  4. The duty repeats every 7 weeks on your Sunday.

🤖 Android — Google Calendar

  1. Choose your name in Phone reminder.
  2. Tap Add to Android Calendar.
  3. Google Calendar opens with the Sunday trash duty.
  4. Tap Save. The event repeats every 7 weeks.
Tip: The website shows the shared household rotation. The calendar reminder is a separate phone reminder, so changing the website rotation does not automatically change an already-created phone event.
